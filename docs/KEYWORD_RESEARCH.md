# Keyword Research

This file separates **evidence** from **assumptions**. No search volume, keyword difficulty, or traffic figure appears here, because none has been verified. We have no access to Google Keyword Planner, Ahrefs, or Semrush data.

## Evidence we have (checked 14 November 2026)

| Topic | What we observed | Limit of this evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Sales tax calculators | Several free sales tax calculators already list Canadian provincial rates, and some list US state rates. | We did not measure search volume or the quality of each competitor page. |
| Concrete calculators | At least ten free concrete calculators from established sites and apps. | Shows the topic is crowded. Says nothing about demand. |
| Sales tax rate confusion | Competitor pages sometimes show different rates for the same province. | Seen on a small number of pages; not a measure of how often users are confused. |

## Assumptions (not yet verified)

- People search for "GST HST calculator," "sales tax calculator by state," "fuel cost calculator," "GPA calculator Canada," and "hourly to annual salary calculator."
- Our calculators can be better than existing ones because they explain the rules, label estimates, and show check dates.
- Demand is higher in the United States than in Canada for some topics, or the reverse. Unknown.
